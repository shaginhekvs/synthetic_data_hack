Case 1: Cultural Banquet Reasoning
Scenario:
In a company boardroom in New York, executives Mei, Jiro, Theo, Yara, and Tariq are seated for an
urgent meeting. Tariq has to sit at an end because of camera placement. Mei and Jiro prefer to sit
side by side. Theo must be between Yara and Tariq. Every adjustment reshapes who faces whom, showing
how social reasoning governs placement.

Reasoning question:
Who ends up between Yara and Tariq under these constraints?

Answer:
Since Mei and Jiro sit together and Tariq is isolated at an end, Theo must be next to Yara to
balance the arrangement.

Case 2: Roundtable Discussion
Scenario:
In Geneva, a diplomatic roundtable features five delegates: Zara, Omar, Arjun, Theo, and Daria. Zara
and Arjun are from rival teams and must not be adjacent. Theo sits immediately to Daria's left. The
moderator insists that Omar face Zara. Every adjustment reshapes who faces whom, showing how social
reasoning governs placement.

Reasoning question:
According to the seating rules, who likely sits immediately to Arjun's left?

Answer:
Because Theo and Daria are required to sit together, the person opposite them (Zara) cannot be
adjacent to Omar.
