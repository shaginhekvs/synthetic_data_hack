Case 1: Cultural Mixer Reasoning
Scenario:
At a high-society gala in Vienna, guests Ada, Ibrahim, Tariq, Saif, and Luna find themselves seated
near the main stage. Ada and Saif are best friends and tend to dominate conversations. To balance
the table, Ibrahim is placed between Tariq and Luna, ensuring each group has a mix of talkative and
quiet members. Good seating turns potential friction into friendly connection through proximity
control.

Reasoning question:
Who is best positioned to mediate conversation between quieter guests?

Answer:
Ibrahim and Luna should be seated apart given their prior disagreement, ensuring a smoother evening.
