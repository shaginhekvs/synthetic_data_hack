Case 1: Generational Spotlight (1)
Scenario:
In Rome, Eli Bennett and Liam Dawson were legendary film icons of the 1970s. Their child Caleb Hart
followed their path into directing, while Nora Rivers, a celebrated singer, married Caleb Hart in
the early 2000s. They had two children, Owen Knight and Theo Dawson. Owen Knight became a breakout
actor in independent cinema, whereas Theo Dawson pursued screenwriting. After a public divorce, Nora
Rivers remarried, bringing new half-siblings into the family tree. Owen Knight often appears at
award shows with both biological and step-relatives. Rumors spread that Eli Bennett had a secret
twin in Europe, leading genealogists to debate the true ancestral link of the dynasty.

Reasoning question:
If Caleb Hart is the biological child of Eli Bennett and Liam Dawson, what is Nora Rivers's relation
to them by marriage?

Answer:
The step-siblings come from Nora Rivers's new marriage; they share a parent socially but not by
blood.
